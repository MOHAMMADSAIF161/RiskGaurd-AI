Return Risk Random Forest Model

RiskGuard AI uses a Random Forest classifier to estimate the probability of an order being returned. The model analyzes current order attributes together with available customer behavior derived from previous orders in the merchant database.

From Prediction to Action

RiskGuard AI converts expected financial impact into a simple merchant-facing priority.

LOW — SAFE

Lower expected return loss. You can process the order normally.

MEDIUM — MAKE CONTACT

Moderate expected loss. You may contact the customer before fulfillment.

HIGH — REVIEW ORDER

Higher expected loss. This order needs additional merchant attention.
